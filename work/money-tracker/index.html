MoneyTracker

A student-friendly budgeting app for Android with a weekly budget, monthly charts, savings goals and shareable summaries.

Download for Android (APK) Android only. Allow “install from unknown sources” when your phone asks.

About the app

Money Tracker helps students keep an eye on where their money goes. Set a weekly budget, log what you spend, and watch monthly charts show the pattern. Savings goals keep a target in view, and a monthly summary can be shared with a tap.

It works offline, so you can log an expense anywhere. If you sign in with Google, your data syncs to the cloud and follows you to a new phone.

Features

  • Weekly budget

    Set a limit for the week and see how much is left.

  • Monthly charts

    Understand your spending at a glance.

  • Savings goals

    Track progress towards what you are saving for.

  • Shareable summaries

    Send your monthly summary to anyone.

  • Offline first

    Works without a connection.

  • Optional cloud sync

    Sign in with Google to back up and sync.
