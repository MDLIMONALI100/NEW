Project overview

Vira Skin Care is a Shopify store concept for a premium skin care brand. I customised the theme so the products, ingredients and brand story take centre stage, and kept the path to checkout short.

I build Shopify stores like this one from Kushtia, Bangladesh, for brands that sell locally and to customers abroad.

The challenge

Skin care shoppers buy on trust and on how clean a brand feels. A cluttered theme or a long checkout works against that, so the design had to be calm, quick, and clear about what each product does.

Technical stack used

  • Shopify
  • Liquid
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript

My role and solutions

  • Adapted the Shopify theme with Liquid and CSS to a restrained, premium look that matches the brand.
  • Structured collections and product pages so benefits, details and the add-to-cart button are all visible without hunting.
  • Kept the checkout path short and tested the store across phone, tablet and desktop.

Business impact and results

  • A calm, uncluttered storefront that reads as premium on the first screen.
  • Short route from product to checkout, which is the part of a Shopify store that matters most for sales.
