Project overview

Extraction is a top-down shooter where you deploy into a random map full of enemies and loot, then reach the green zone and survive to escape. Get out alive and you keep your loot; die and you lose everything you brought. It started as a solo game and now includes online player-versus-player matches and an Android app.

I build apps and games like this from Kushtia, Bangladesh, and ship them so anyone in the world can open them in a browser or on their phone.

The challenge

A real-time multiplayer game has to feel responsive on a phone's mobile connection, while staying cheap to run. I also wanted every piece of art and sound generated in code, so the game stays small and loads fast.

Technical stack used

  • TypeScript
  • Phaser 3
  • Vite
  • Vitest
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Durable Objects
  • Capacitor (Android)

My role and solutions

  • Designed and programmed the whole game: combat, enemy AI, loot, hideout, bombs and the raid loop, written as plain, tested logic separate from the drawing layer.
  • Built online multiplayer rooms (free-for-all or teams, up to 15 players) with one Cloudflare Durable Object per room and client-side prediction and interpolation.
  • Packaged the game as a signed Android app with Capacitor, and shipped it from the same deploy as the web version.

Business impact and results

  • An automated suite of more than 240 tests covers the game rules, so new features don't quietly break old ones.
  • The Android app is roughly 1.3 MB because art and sound are generated in code.
  • No database to run: solo progress saves in the browser and multiplayer rooms hold no stored data.
